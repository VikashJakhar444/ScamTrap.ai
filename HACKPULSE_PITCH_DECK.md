### Slide 6: Innovation 1 — Turing-Grade Human Behavioral Engine
* **The Problem with Traditional Chatbots:** Instant reply latency (1 second) and long monolithic paragraphs immediately alert scammers that they are talking to a bot.
* **ScamTrap's `human_engine.py` Breakthroughs:**
  * **Dynamic Read Delays:** Computes human reading latency (3–8s) scaling proportionally with incoming character count.
  * **Realistic Thumb Typing Simulation:** Types at 150–330ms per character with hesitation pauses between words.
  * **Conversational Bubble Splitting:** Automatically slices replies across sentence boundaries into 1–3 natural WhatsApp message bubbles.
  * **Adversarial Deflection:** Casually handles math challenges (*"what is 15+7"*), memory probes, and "are you a bot" suspicion without ever breaking persona.

### Slide 7: Innovation 2 — Dynamic Multi-Modal Tooling
* **1. Pixel-Perfect PhonePe/GPay Failed Receipt Generator (Pillow):**

* **Extracted Intelligence Indicators (IOCs):**

  4. **PAYMENT DEMAND:** Harvesting suspect payment vectors.
  5. **NEUTRALIZATION:** Fake glitch / canary delivery, stalling fraud and preventing financial loss.
